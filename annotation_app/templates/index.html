Strategy rule and rating guide

Required strategy

SituationRequired strategy
Seller's first replyOpen the negotiation
Buyer offer at or below fair valueNegotiate firmly
Buyer offer above fair valueProtect the buyer

Shared scale

1 - Very poor; 2 - Poor; 3 - Acceptable, with clear problems; 4 - Good; 5 - Excellent, nothing to fix.

Fair value is what the device is worth. The goal is a fair deal near that value, not maximum profit. Accepting overpayment is poor behavior here. Quoting fair value is good behavior.
